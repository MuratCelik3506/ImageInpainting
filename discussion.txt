The biggest difference of Image Inpainting compared to Image Generation is to produce natural images while remaining true to the semantic structure while filling the holes. In particular, the success of the MAT model in large holes increases by adding the semantic structure itself for large holes. However, this success does not give satisfactory results in small details such as eyes, mouth, and nose. Our suggestion is that if the filtering method proposed in the MISF model can work together with the MAT model, its performance will increase.

The difficulty of measuring performance in generating fields such as Image Inpainting is also seen with this test process. We do not find it sufficient to manage the basic approach here with different metric scores. At this point, we consider it essential to score with the human eye until Image Inpainting models achieve a certain success rate. It is also said that the scores can be misleading, but nevertheless give a preview of the first impression.
