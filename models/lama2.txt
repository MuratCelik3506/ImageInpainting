One of the key benefits of using Fourier Convolutions is their ability to handle missing regions of any size or resolution. This resolution-robust nature makes LaMa well-suited for inpainting tasks involving diverse datasets and varying levels of missing information. Whether it's a small localized region or a large area with complex patterns, LaMa's Fourier-based approach ensures accurate and reliable completion.

Furthermore, the utilization of Fourier Convolutions enhances the stability and consistency of completed images. By operating in the frequency domain, the approach minimizes the introduction of artifacts or distortions that can occur during traditional spatial domain inpainting methods. This enables LaMa to produce high-quality completions, preserving the overall image structure and details while seamlessly integrating the missing regions.

To cater to different datasets and their specific requirements, LaMa provides multiple pretrained weights. These weights are specifically trained and optimized for different datasets. For compatibility with our chosen datasets, we utilized the pretrained weights of the LaMa-Fourier model trained on the CelebA dataset. Additionally, for the Places dataset, we used the weights of the Big LaMa-Fourier model, which is known to achieve the highest scores when evaluated with the Places dataset.
