In our study, we use the CelebAMask-HQ dataset consisting of 30,000 images and the Places365 dataset, from which we received 4000 images with 40 different labels. Selected labels are apartment building (outdoor), barn, building facade, cabin (outdoor), campus, carrousel, chalet, church (outdoor), cottage, courthouse, courtyard, downtown, fire escape, forest (broad leaf), garage (outdoor), gazebo (exterior), golf course, hospital, hotel (outdoor), house, hunting lodge (outdoor), ice shelf, inn (outdoor), library (outdoor), manufactured home, mausoleum, mountain path, museum (outdoor), pagoda, parking garage (outdoor), porch, school house, shed, shop front, skyscraper, synagogue (outdoor), temple (Asia), tower, wet bar, and wind farm.

To examine the various challenges posed by different masks for CelebAMask-HQ, we include masks that target specific areas such as the face, hair, and skin. We also randomly prepare 8 different percentages (20\%, 30\%, 40\%, 50\%, 60\%, 70\%, 80\%, 90\%) masks for each image. We randomly prepare 4 different percentages (20\%, 40\%, 60\%, 80\%) masks for Places365 images. This results in a total extraction of 330,000 images per model for the CelebAMask-HQ dataset, and a total of 16,000 images per model for the Places365 dataset, taking into account the various masks used.

The models employed in our study have been described in previous sections. We utilized pre-existing models that had been pretrained using the relevant dataset. No additional training was conducted for these models during the course of our study.

The evaluation of images involve the application of various metrics to assess their quality, allowing for comparisons between different models. The metrics employe in our study include l1 loss, l2 loss, PSNR, LPIPS, and SSIM.

Given that the models used for inpainting require GPU resources, we conduct the model testing with the support of GPUs provided by Google Colab.
