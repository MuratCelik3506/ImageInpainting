Generative Image Inpainting with Contextual Attention

The article on Generative Image Inpainting introduces an innovative approach that utilizes deep generative models to enhance the accuracy of filling in missing parts of images. This technique explicitly leverages the surrounding image features as a reference during the network training phase, resulting in improved predictions. The model itself is a feedforward, fully convolutional neural network capable of effectively handling images that contain multiple holes of different sizes and random locations during testing. To evaluate the effectiveness of this approach, the researchers studied various datasets, including CelebA and CelebA-HQ for faces, DTD for textures, as well as ImageNet and Places2 for a diverse range of natural images.
