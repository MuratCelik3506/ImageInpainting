Patch-Based Image Inpainting with Generative Adversarial Networks

The patch-based approach \cite{demir2018patchbased} allows for fine-grained control and generation of high-quality content that seamlessly integrates with the surrounding context. One of the key contributions of the article is the use of GANs to facilitate the generation of inpainted patches. The generator network is trained to produce visually plausible patches that match the local context, while the discriminator network distinguishes between real and generated patches, providing feedback for improving the generator's performance.Additionally, the research develops a patch-based loss function that directs training and promotes spatial coherence between inpainted patches and adjacent picture regions.  By considering local patches instead of the entire image, the proposed method achieves better preservation of fine details and textures.
