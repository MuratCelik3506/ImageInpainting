During our analysis, we found that the model achieved success in specific score calculations for annotation masks. However, when random masks were employed, we observed significant variations in the success rates across different metrics calculations. Interestingly, we found that while the scores indicated success, a visual inspection of the CelebAMask-HQ outputs  revealed issues, as you can see in examples of outputs for LaMa model. The images generated by the LaMa model did not align well with the human visual system. The image completion results were frequently inadequate, even when high scores were attained.

Then, we compared the performance of the LaMa model on the Places365 dataset against CelebAMask-HQ. Under visually controlled conditions, the LaMa model produced better completion results for the Lama Places dataset. However, when comparing the scores, it was not the most successful model.

Furthermore, it is worth noting that the scores achieved for both datasets fell short of the scores specified in the article in which the method is presented. This shortcoming is probably due to the variety of masks affecting the performance of the model.
