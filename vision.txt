As a result of the experience we gained with this study, we gained great knowledge and insight in the field of image inpainting. These experiences gave us information about the directions in which this field could go. We are of the opinion that for the following period, works in the field of image inpainting can be done with style determination and transformations from text to painting. We hope to contribute to these interesting areas with our own models in the future.

With recent developments, generative ai models have increased their popularity for both picture and text and different fields. With this popularity, generative ai has reached a position that is followed not only by people working in this field but by everyone. We want our name to be among those who build the future with generative ai studies, which we see as an important checkpoint when building the future. We want this study to be a starting point for us to enter this field, and we want to carry out studies in many points of generative ai fields, not limited to the field of image inpainting.
