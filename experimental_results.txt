Image inpainting is the process of completing the missing parts of the image by preserving its semantic integrity and giving importance to naturalness. To measure the performance of image inpainting models, a comparison must be made between the image whose holes are produced and the original image. In this comparison, we can define success as comprehending the semantic structures of the painting, preserving its naturalness and producing outputs close to the original. The difficulty is that this comparison only makes sense for this comparison method. For this reason, success measurements are made with different metrics in image inpainting studies.

In this study, we recorded separate scores for each output using 5 different metrics. To ensure the accuracy of the scoring, we recorded the average of the scores of the outputs produced by the same method for each dataset. Statistics of the CelebAMask-HQ dataset can be seen in Table of CelebAMask-HQ scores, and the statistics of the Places365 dataset in Table of Places365 scores. The best result in the 3 models for each output method and each scoring is indicated in bold.

As can be seen in the tables, while one model is considered successful for a score, another model appears to be successful in a different scoring. This proves that there cannot be a single scoring metric that can be used in this field.
